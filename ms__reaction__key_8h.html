Detailed Description

The stable 64-bit key of a reaction used by the master scheduler (D and X records, replay, the key-collision check): FNV-1a of the reactor's full name, mixed with the reaction number.

Shared by the worker dispatch (reactor_threaded.c) and the direct execution of a downstream reaction (reactor_common.c), so both name a reaction alike.
